About APU Karting Club


Club Mission:

To give every APU student real seat time in a real kart — teaching race craft, car control, and race-day discipline in a club that's run by students, for students.

Club Vision:

To be the starting grid for Malaysia's next generation of motorsport talent, turning curious first-timers into confident, competitive drivers.

Objective:

  • Open the track to everyone
    No experience needed — we train members from their first lap to their first podium.
  • Race safely, race properly
    Every session runs on clear rules, briefed marshals, and proper safety gear.
  • Compete beyond campus
    We put members forward for external karting events and inter-university competitions.
  • Build a paddock, not just a club
    Karting is the excuse — the community, the crew, and the friendships are the point.
