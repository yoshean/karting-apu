Rules & Regulations

Official track safety rules, flag signals, and racing conduct guidelines.

Official Circuit Flag Signals

Marshals communicate track conditions via flags. Memorize their meanings prior to race day.

Green Flag — Track Clear & Racing Live

Session has commenced. Full throttle and safe overtaking permitted throughout the circuit.

Yellow Flag — Hazard Ahead / Caution

Obstruction ahead. Immediately reduce speed by 30%, and strictly NO overtaking until past the incident zone.

Red Flag — Immediate Session Suspension

Critical danger. Slow down immediately in a controlled manner and return safely to the pit lane.

Blue Flag — Yield to Faster Kart

A faster driver or race leader is directly behind. Hold a predictable racing line and allow clean passage.

Black Flag — Disqualification / Pit Summon

Indicates deliberate bumping or safety violation. Enter the pit box immediately for steward review.
🏁

Chequered Flag — Race Concluded

Cross the start/finish line, ease off the accelerator, complete a cooldown lap, and enter the pit lane.

The Four Golden Rules of the Paddock

Zero tolerance for dangerous behavior. Respect your fellow APU racers.

RULE #1

Never Exit the Kart on Track

If your kart spins out or stalls, stay seated with your helmet on. Raise both hands above your head so circuit marshals can see and assist you safely.

RULE #2

Zero Contact (No Bumping)

Go-karts are high-speed open-wheel machines. Deliberate rear-ending or squeezing drivers into tire barriers results in immediate black flag eviction.

RULE #3

Single-Pedal Technique

Left foot controls the brake, right foot controls the accelerator. Never press both pedals simultaneously, which burns the clutch and damages the brake rotor.

RULE #4

Pit Lane Walking Pace

Marshals and students walk through the pit area. Speeds exceeding 10 km/h in the pit lane will incur a mandatory 10-second timing penalty.
