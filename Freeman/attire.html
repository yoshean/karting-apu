Attire & Equipment

Compulsory driver protective gear, racing standards and prohibited clothing.

Compulsory Safety Essentials

Every driver must meet these criteria before entering the pit lane.

Full Face Helmet

Helmets are provided free of charge or you may opt to bring your own, Only Snell certified helmet with a functional and visor is acceptable.

Closed Toe Flat Shoes

Racing boots, Sneakers, sports trainers or with thin flat rubber soles for pedal tactile feedback. Sandals, Crocs, slippers, or open footwear are strictly prohibited.

Full-Length Pants

Comfortable jeans or heavy track pants. Because kart engines and radiators sit inches away from your hips, shorts are strictly forbidden on the circuit.

Balaclava (Head Sock)

Required under all circuit rental helmets for sweat absorption and hygiene. Balaclavas are distributed to club members or available at track registration for RM 5.

Racing Gloves

Gripped tactical or karting gloves reduce vibration fatigue over 15-minute heats and protect knuckles during high-G cornering. Strongly recommended for racing.

Rib Protector Vest

Kart bucket seats experience up to 2.5G lateral cornering loads. Rib protectors absorb impact against the fiberglass seat shell and are provided on-site upon request.

Paddock Dress Code: Do's & Don'ts

A quick reference to avoid being turned away at track scrutineering.

✅ Recommended ❌ Strictly Prohibited
Fitted t-shirts, athletic long-sleeve jerseys, or racing overalls Loose & long scarves or neck ties - (hazard near axle)
Long hair neatly tied, braided, and tucked firmly inside jacket Untied long flowing hair extending past the helmet collar
Tightly laced sneakers, trainers, or flat driving shoes Crocs in sport mode, flip flops, high heels, or canvas slip-ons
Secured eyeglasses or contact lenses with helmet eye-port clearance Loose hanging jewelry, long necklaces, or dangling keychains
Personal FIA/CIK-FIA approved racing suit, gloves, and boots Short pants, three-quarter capris, or skirts/dresses on track
