Sponsors & Circuit Partners

Collaborating with industry-leading tracks, motorsport apparel brands, and APU Student Affairs.

PRIMARY CIRCUIT PARTNER

Aylezo Speedway Circuit

Aylezo Speedway is our official home track. Spearheaded by Malaysian race driver Zen Low, Aylezo offers a challenging 11-turn layout, cutting-edge Sodi RT8 270cc rental karts, and computerized MyLaps timing loops.

Location: Shah Alam, Selangor (35 mins from APU)

Member Benefit: 25% discounted booking rates for registered APU Karting members

Equipment Provided: High-performance helmets, balaclavas, telemetry timing printouts
